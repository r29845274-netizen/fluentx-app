## Short description
Improve English speaking, writing, vocabulary, grammar and interview confidence with AI-powered practice.

## Full description
FluentX is an AI-powered English communication coach built for learners who want structured, practical improvement in speaking, writing, vocabulary, grammar, listening and interview communication.

Start with a placement experience, follow a personalized learning path, practise key language skills, and track progress over time. FluentX combines guided lessons, practice activities, AI-assisted feedback, progress tracking and communication-focused coaching in one app.

Key features include:
- Personalized English learning path
- Vocabulary and grammar practice
- Listening and writing exercises
- AI-assisted communication practice
- Speaking and interview practice
- Placement-based learning guidance
- Progress, goals, streaks and achievements
- Premium subscription options through Google Play

AI-generated feedback is provided as an educational aid and may not always be perfect. FluentX is designed for language-learning and communication-practice purposes.
