Delete your FluentX account

This page is the external account-deletion pathway for the FluentX English learning app.

Delete inside the app

Open FluentX → Profile → Settings → Delete Account and confirm deletion.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have access to FluentX, use the deletion-request contact/form that will be published on this page before Play Store release.

Production action required: replace the placeholder below with a monitored support form or deletion-request email link before publishing.

Request account deletion

What is deleted

  • Your FluentX account identity.
  • Learning progress, goals, achievements and practice history linked to your account.
  • Writing, AI-practice, interview and placement records linked to your account.
  • Stored notification/device tokens linked to your account where applicable.

Retention

If any information must be retained for security, fraud prevention, legal or regulatory reasons, that retention must be disclosed in the published FluentX Privacy Policy.
