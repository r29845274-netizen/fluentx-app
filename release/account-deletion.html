Delete your FluentX account

This page explains how users of the FluentX English-learning app can permanently delete their account and associated app data.

Delete inside the app

Open FluentX → Profile → Settings → Delete Account and confirm deletion.

Delete without reinstalling the app

If you no longer have FluentX installed, contact FluentX Support using the public support contact shown on the FluentX store listing or official support page and request permanent account deletion. Include the email address used for your FluentX account so the request can be verified.

What is deleted

  • Your FluentX account identity.
  • Learning progress, goals, achievements and practice history linked to your account.
  • Writing, AI-practice, interview and placement records linked to your account.
  • Stored notification or device tokens linked to your account where applicable.

What may be retained

Limited information may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, accounting, legal or regulatory obligations. Any such retention must follow the published FluentX Privacy Policy and applicable law.

Subscriptions

Deleting your FluentX account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions from Google Play before deleting your account if you do not want future renewals.
