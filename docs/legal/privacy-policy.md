# FluentX Privacy Policy — publication draft

Status: **Legal review required before public launch.**

## Data handled
FluentX may process account identifiers, profile/onboarding choices, learning progress, written practice, AI-practice transcripts, support requests, device/session metadata, subscription entitlement state, crash diagnostics, and usage analytics.

## Purposes
Data is used to authenticate users, provide learning features, preserve progress, prevent abuse, operate AI practice, support users, administer subscriptions, secure the service, and diagnose failures.

## Processors
The current architecture can involve Supabase (auth/database/functions), Firebase (Crashlytics/analytics/messaging), RevenueCat (subscription entitlements), OpenAI (AI practice/writing scoring when configured), and platform app stores.

## Security and access
Row-level security, server-side authorization, admin MFA, audited privileged access, rate limits, and device/session controls reduce unauthorized access.

## Retention and deletion
Account deletion is supported through the delete-account backend. Operational, security, billing, or legal records may require limited retention where permitted by law.
